# AER research guideline

## Original hardware evidence and independent development

Arcade Experience Research (AER) seeks to understand the behavior of original
arcade games and hardware. Research must originate from original game
executables, original hardware, original documentation, or independently
captured runtime behavior. Third-party interpretations are not authoritative
implementation sources.

## Evidence hierarchy

### Tier 1 — Original hardware

Direct observation or measurement of authentic arcade hardware, including:

- original drive-board output;
- motor behavior;
- steering resistance;
- original cabinet diagnostics; and
- physical force measurements.

### Tier 2 — Original game runtime

Commands generated by the original arcade executable and captured before
loader or emulator interpretation, including:

- original drive-board requests;
- native game state;
- original hardware-interface traffic; and
- original executable behavior.

### Tier 3 — Original documentation

Authentic manufacturer documentation, including:

- SEGA service manuals;
- hardware specifications;
- drive-board documentation; and
- original calibration procedures.

### Tier 4 — Third-party runtime observation

Emulators and loaders may be used as research instruments. Examples include
Lindbergh Loader, XEMU, and other arcade runtime environments. They may expose
original game behavior, but their translations and approximations are not
automatically original hardware behavior.

### Tier 5 — Third-party interpretations

FFBArcadePlugin, other FFB modifications, reconstructed force models, and
community-derived mappings may be documented for historical comparison. They
must not supply implementation semantics, coefficients, algorithms, or force
mappings.

## Lindbergh Loader boundary

Lindbergh Loader is the primary AER research candidate for OutRun 2 SP SDX. Its
research purpose is to provide access to original game-generated hardware
communication.

Research should observe commands at the earliest reliable interception
boundary. The loader's SDL force effects are not authentic arcade force
measurements. Any loader modification, substitution, filtering, or synthesized
response must be documented.

## Independent validation

Original command bytes do not automatically establish physical meaning.
Command interpretation requires independent evidence. Where possible, validate
it using:

- original arcade hardware;
- original manufacturer documentation;
- controlled original-game observations; and
- repeatable native runtime captures.

Do not promote uncertain interpretations into verified arcade behavior.

## Implementation separation

Preserve this boundary:

```text
ORIGINAL OBSERVATION
    ↓
INDEPENDENT SEMANTIC VALIDATION
    ↓
OUR OWN BEHAVIORAL MODEL
    ↓
HYP36rforce / AER PRESENTATION
```

Do not import third-party source code, algorithms, constants, force mappings,
tuning values, or reconstructed effect models.

## Documentation requirements

Every AER finding should identify:

- original game and revision;
- original hardware platform;
- observation method;
- runtime or loader used;
- original versus translated data;
- known modifications;
- evidence source;
- confidence; and
- remaining validation.

Use these confidence classifications consistently:

- **VERIFIED** — meaning is established strongly enough for the stated use.
- **NEEDS VALIDATION** — credible evidence exists, but meaning or lifecycle is
  not sufficiently proven.
- **UNKNOWN** — observed evidence has no established meaning.
- **UNSUITABLE** — evidence is not stable or meaningful enough for the stated
  use.

Do not describe loader-generated FFB as original arcade FFB, inferred torque as
measured torque, or third-party interpretations as original SEGA
specifications.

## Clean-room development

AER implementations must be independently developed. External projects may be
reviewed to understand the research landscape, but their implementations must
not determine ours. When similar behavior is independently discovered,
preserve our original evidence and development history.

## Release isolation

AER research does not automatically become a HYP36rforce feature. Experimental
behavior may be promoted only after:

- validated evidence;
- documented semantics;
- independent implementation;
- controlled UAT;
- safety validation; and
- release approval.

## Core principle

Original arcade evidence is the research foundation. Loaders and emulators are
observation instruments. Third-party interpretations are not implementation
authorities. Unknown remains unknown.
